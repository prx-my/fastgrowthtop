Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about roof restoration, hail damage, or insurance in Texas? We have answers.

Hail damage often cannot be seen from the ground. Hail impacts create subtle bruises in shingle asphalt, fracturing the fiberglass mat and knocking loose protective granules. Over several months, sunlight degrades the exposed underlayment leading to major leaks. Texas Roofers provides a 100% free attic and roof inspection with photographic proof.

Yes! In Texas, wind and hail damage are typically covered perils on standard homeowner insurance policies. Our team works directly alongside your insurance adjuster, submitting detailed measurements, eagle-view satellite reports, and photos to ensure you receive complete replacement coverage without fighting insurance paperwork yourself.
