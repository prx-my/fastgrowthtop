01 / Wall

Foam that fills every gap.

Cold air finds the gaps around studs and plates. Spray foam expands into the cavity, fills the corners and seals them. Less draft, more comfort, and a house that holds its heat.

Attics filled to depth.

Spray foam is not the only tool. Blown-in cellulose fills the attic over the joists, with vents and baffles kept clear. It is the quiet work that shows up in warmer rooms upstairs.

Insulation, barns, concrete and remodeling

Residential and commercial work, built for Michigan winters

  • Spray foam insulation

    Open- and closed-cell foam for walls, rim joists and rooflines. Closed cell adds an air and moisture barrier on metal-sided barns.

  • Blown-in insulation

    Cellulose and loose fill for attics, from new builds to older homes that need topping up.

  • Pole barns

    Posts, trusses and steel, built to work, with foam on the inside of the shell when you want to use it year round.

  • Garage buildings

    Garages and shop buildings framed and insulated by the same crew.

  • Concrete flatwork

    Driveways, aprons and shop floors, poured, struck and troweled.

  • Remodeling

    Rooms that feel finished, with the insulation done right while the walls are open.
