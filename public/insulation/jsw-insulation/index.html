Our Insulation & Building Services

Residential and commercial work across Osceola County and Northern Michigan

  • Closed-Cell Spray Foam

    Dense foam that adds an air and moisture barrier and stiffens the wall. The go-to for metal-sided pole barns and shops.

  • Open-Cell Spray Foam

    Lighter and lower cost per inch. Fills walls and rooflines in homes and additions and helps quiet sound.

  • Pole Barn & Shop Insulation

    Walls, gable ends and ceilings foamed so your barn, shop or garage holds heat in winter and stays dry.

  • Cellulose Attic Insulation

    Blown-in cellulose and loose-fill for attics, from new builds to older homes that need topping up.

  • Pole Building Construction

    We build pole buildings and garage buildings too, so one crew handles the frame and the insulation.

  • Concrete, Additions & Remodeling

    Concrete flat work, additions and remodeling, with general contracting available for the whole job.
